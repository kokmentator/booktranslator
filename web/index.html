Settings

What it does

  • Your original book is on the right, the translation on the left, side by side — any language pair (set per book in data\books.json).
  • Click into the translation and rewrite it. Press Enter to save — every change is saved automatically.
  • Select a sentence on either side and its match on the other side lights up and scrolls into view.

The books (tabs, top-left)

  • Switch between imported books any time. Each keeps its own translation, chat, and notes.
  • Add a book with the importer: node server\import\importBook.js mybook manuscript.docx --title "…"
  • Use the chapter dropdown to jump around a book.

Finding a better word

  • Right-click (or double-click) any word in the translation → a popup of options: your locked glossary terms, a thesaurus (English targets only), and the AI's in-context picks. Click one to swap it.

The side panel (right)

  • Suggestions — the spots where the translation wasn't sure; pick the wording you want.
  • Chat — discuss a passage, then click Apply to selection to use it. (Opens automatically when you click a paragraph.)

The other buttons (top bar)

  • − 100% + — make the text bigger or smaller (also Ctrl +, Ctrl −).
  • Export — make a clean Word (.docx) file of the translation.
  • Panel — hide or show the side panel.

Where your work is saved

  • Every edit saves instantly to data\<book>\project.json (with timestamped backups in data\<book>\backups\).
  • Chat history: data\<book>\chat.jsonl
  • Exported Word files: data\<book>\exports\
  • House-style rules, glossary & idioms: data\style\ (shared) and data\<book>\style\ (per book).
  • Your original manuscript files are never changed.

Starting it (and on another PC)

  • Windows: double-click start.cmd in the project folder. Any OS: npm start. It opens at http://localhost:4319.
  • On a different PC: the whole app travels in its own folder. Make sure Node.js is installed, then start it the same way — it works from any path or drive letter.

Turning on Claude's live help (chat + synonyms)

  • Open a Claude Code session in this app's folder and type /engine to answer waiting requests once, or /loop 30s /engine to keep answering automatically while you work.
  • The command travels with the app, so it's the same on every PC.
  • Or use a different AI on the AI provider tab — then the app answers by itself with no session needed.
  • Without either, your edits, the thesaurus, and the glossary still work instantly — only chat/synonym replies wait.

Pick a provider, paste an API key, then switch it on for Synonyms and/or Chat. Anything left off keeps using the built-in Claude session. The key is saved on this PC/USB in data\engine.config.json.

Free options: Zhipu GLM Flash, Google Gemini Flash, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Cerebras (all marked FREE). Cheap paid: DeepSeek-V4, Kimi K2.7, GLM-5.2. Your chat history is saved the same way, whichever AI answers. For China endpoints, override the Base URL (open.bigmodel.cn / api.moonshot.cn).

The author

Feed the translator everything that shapes this author's voice: who they are, their background and era, how they write (sentence habits, humour, darkness, favourite images), what they would never say, and what must survive translation at all costs. The AI reads this before every translation, suggestion and chat reply for this book.
