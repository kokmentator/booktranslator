# How NOT to sound machine-translated

- Ban filler intensifiers the source doesn't have: "truly", "simply", "utterly".
- No hedging the author didn't write: "perhaps", "it seemed that", "somewhat".
- Vary sentence openings; machine drafts start too many sentences the same way.
- Contractions are allowed wherever the register is conversational.
- Prefer concrete verbs over verb + noun constructions ("decided" not "made a decision").
