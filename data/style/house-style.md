# House style — the non-negotiable rules

These rules are injected into every AI translation, chat reply and synonym request.
Replace them with your own; this file is the heart of the whole system — the longer
you translate, the more of your taste lives here.

- Match the register of the source. Never elevate plain language into formal prose.
- Preserve paragraph structure exactly: one source paragraph → one target paragraph.
- Keep sentence rhythm: if the author writes short, punchy sentences, do not merge them.
- Translate meaning and voice, not words. An idiomatic phrase gets an idiomatic phrase.
- Proper names: keep them in the original unless the glossary says otherwise.
- Do not censor, soften, or "improve" the source. If the author is crude, be crude.
- When genuinely unsure between two good renderings, prefer the plainer one.
